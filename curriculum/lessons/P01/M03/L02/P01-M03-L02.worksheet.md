## Checkpoint 1 (15 minutes)

**Questions:**

1. Why does the 50-character guideline exist, specifically? What breaks when subjects are too long?

2. You're committing a change that adds a "mark task complete" checkbox to the Helm task list. Write a subject line for it in Conventional Commits format.

**Answers:**

1. Primarily, we keep commit subject lines to 50 characters or less to allow the `git log --oneline` command to display properly without ellipses or cutting off potentially important information. Effective commits should be concise otherwise it may indicate the commit is doing to much and needs to be split.

2. feat(tasks): add mark complete checkbox
