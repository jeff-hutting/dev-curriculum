**Professor Feedback:**

Outstanding responses! Your understanding of constructive feedback and tone is excellent.

**Question 1: Starting with Appreciation**

You've identified both the psychological benefits (reduced defensiveness) and the cultural benefits (collaborative environment). This shows you understand code review is as much about human dynamics as technical correctness.

One additional benefit: Starting with appreciation also **calibrates expectations**. When you say "Great work on the API integration!" before noting issues, it signals "This is fundamentally good work with some fixable problems," not "This is fundamentally flawed." This is a textbook example of constructive feedback. You completely transformed the tone from attacking to collaborative:

**What makes it effective:**

   - First version: Invites exploration
   - Second version: Commands action

**3. Acknowledging tradeoffs:** "It may be the better option"
   - Uses "may" instead of "is," showing humility
   - Implies there might be valid reasons for the current approach

**4. Explicit invitation:** "Let me know what you think!"
   - Explicitly requests their perspective
   - Shows respect for their expertise
