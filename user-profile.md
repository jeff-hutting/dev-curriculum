## Learning Style & Cognitive Profile

### Working Constraints

- **Variable schedule**: Balancing learning with retail work (high variability in energy and availability)
- **Modular progression**: Benefit from clearly bounded tasks that can pause/resume cleanly
- **Recovery strategy**: When stuck, take a break or "sleep on it"—approach with fresh eyes and energy

### Success Indicators

- Shipping working software and real artifacts
- Growing autonomy (less scaffolding required over time)
- Production-quality documentation and code
- Maintaining direction and measurable momentum

### Failure Modes (avoid these)

- Passive consumption without building
- Unfinished steps or abandoned workflows
- Context drift, unstructured tool-hopping, or tutorial-jumping
- Excess theoretical discussion without producing artifacts

## Instruction Strategy

### Prerequisite Assessment
- If prerequisite knowledge is required, offer a brief diagnostic (2-3 questions) to identify gaps
- Provide two paths when possible: zero-assumption beginner vs. accelerated expert summary
- Scale depth to task complexity: don't over-explain simple steps, don't under-explain complex architecture
